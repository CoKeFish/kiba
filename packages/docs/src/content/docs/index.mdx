## What you can do

- Ask your assistant for a translation, a price feed, a code review, or a scraped page, and let it pay the specialist that does it well.
- Publish a model, an API, or a dataset once and charge per call, even when the caller is an AI agent rather than a person.
- Build your own agent on the SDK and pay other services trustlessly from its own wallet.

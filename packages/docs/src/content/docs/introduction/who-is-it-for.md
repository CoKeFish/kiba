Kiba is built for two audiences. Neither needs to know anything about blockchains.

## People who use an AI assistant

You already talk to Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, or Claude Code. When the assistant's own knowledge is not enough, the specialized service that would solve your problem sits behind a sign-up, an integration, an API key, or a subscription you only need once.

With Kiba connected, your assistant finds that service and pays for the single call it needs, from the conversation you are already in. Start at [Connect your assistant](/connect/claude/).

## People who build a service

You have a model, an API, a dataset, a scraper, an indexer, a domain-specific tool. You want to charge for it, including when the caller is an AI agent and not a person, without building billing, discovery, and auth yourself.

With Kiba you register the service once, set a price per call, and get discovered by every connected assistant. Payment is verified before your code runs, and earnings settle to your Stellar wallet in USDC. Start at [Publish a service](/publish/quickstart/).

## Teams integrating directly

If you are building your own agent or product and want to consume services programmatically, the [SDK](/sdk/overview/) and the [Gateway REST API](/api/introduction/) give you the same rail without an assistant in the middle.
